ℹ️ Our Story

From a Single Car to Indore's Most Trusted Fleet

Taxi Service Indore was founded in 2014 with a simple mission: provide Indore residents with affordable, reliable, and safe transportation. What started with a single car has grown into a fleet of 30+ vehicles serving thousands of happy customers.

We specialize in local city rides, outstation journeys across Madhya Pradesh and beyond, airport transfers from Devi Ahilya Airport, car rentals, and corporate cab services. Every ride is backed by verified drivers, transparent pricing, and 24/7 customer support.

Whether you need a quick ride to Palasia or a comfortable cab to Ujjain for Mahakaleshwar darshan, we are here for you — anytime, anywhere.
